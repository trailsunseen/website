Questions people ask

How much does a trip cost?

It depends on your dates, how many of you are coming and the stay. You get an exact price with your free plan, and there's nothing to pay until you're sure.

Is my dog too big, too old or too energetic?

Tell us about them on the call. We choose stays and outings to suit your dog, whatever their size, age or energy.

Can we bring our kids too?

Yes. Add children under Travellers and we'll plan the days for them as well.

What isn't included?

Your dog's own food and treats (bring what they're used to), and anything not listed on the trip page.

What if my dog falls ill on the trip?

We have a vet we know near each stop, and we change the plan straight away. Your dog comes first, every time.
