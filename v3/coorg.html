Day by day

Day 1Pickup and settle in

We pick you up at your door. An easy drive with water, sniff and stretch stops away from highway traffic. Settle in at a coffee estate stay.

Day 2Trail walk and stream swim

A slow morning walk through the coffee, a swim in the stream if your dog likes water, and a lazy verandah afternoon.

Day 3Ridge sunrise, free day

Up early for the ridge at sunrise, then a free day at your dog's pace. Nothing is compulsory.

Day 4Breakfast, then home

A relaxed breakfast, then the drive home with the same breaks. We drop you at your door.
