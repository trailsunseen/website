Travelling with a dog usually means a dozen phone calls, a lot of hoping, and a stay that turns out to mean “pets on the lawn only”. We go and check, so you don't have to.

Paw-approved

  1. 1

    Stays we've walked through ourselves.

    We visit every stay to check the fences, the floors and how the staff are with dogs. Your dog sleeps in your room, with you.

  2. 2

    Every meal, sorted.

    Breakfast to dinner is planned for the whole trip. No hunting for a dog-friendly table when everyone's hungry.

  3. 3

    Outings planned for four legs.

    Each day is shaped around your dog's energy, not a sightseeing checklist. We check the water before we ever suggest a swim.

  4. 4

    Your own car and driver.

    A car just for your family, from your door and back. Your dog rides with you, never in a crate or the boot.

  5. 5

    A vet on call, day and night.

    Someone who picks up at 2 a.m. If your dog needs to be seen, your driver takes you straight there.
