Good questions

Before you ask

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and a real person will reply.

Which dogs can come?add

Most dogs, big or small, puppies to seniors. Tell us about yours and we'll be honest about whether the trip suits them, and shape it if it needs to.

How much does it cost?add

It depends on your dates, how many of you are travelling and the stay you pick. Send us an enquiry and we'll share a clear, all-in price.

How do I book?add

Send us an enquiry. We'll call or message you, agree the dates and the plan, and send you a payment link to confirm.

Can we bring more than one dog?add

Usually, yes. Tell us how many and how they get along, and we'll pick a stay with room for all of them.

What if my dog gets unwell?add

We know the nearest vet at every stop and how to get there. And if your dog needs a slow day, the plan changes.

Does my dog need anything before the trip?add

Up-to-date vaccinations and a tick and flea treatment. We'll send a short checklist when you book.

Can the kids come too?add

Of course. Kids are welcome on dog trips, and trips designed for families with young children are coming soon.

We live in Hyderabad. Can we join?add

Hyderabad routes are coming soon. Send an enquiry and you'll hear first.

What if we need to cancel?add

See our cancellation policy. If your dog falls ill before the trip, talk to us and we'll do our best to move your dates.

Ready when your dog is.

Tell us about them and we'll shape a trip around them. A real person replies within a day.
