Our story

We built the trips we wanted to take.

Two families, one very opinionated golden retriever, and a lot of weekends we didn't go anywhere.

India is full of places most people never see: a coffee estate that's been in one family for a century, a stream that only the locals swim in, a ridge where the whole valley opens up at sunrise. We wanted to see them with the people (and the dog) we love most.

But travelling with a dog, or with small kids, turns every trip into a project. Will the stay really allow dogs? Where do we stop on the way? What if something goes wrong at night, three hours from home?

So we started Trails Unseen. We find the places, check them ourselves, plan the pace around your dog and your kids, and handle every detail, so all you have to do is enjoy it.

Discovery is the product. Travel is the medium.
