Coorg · 3 nights · from Bengaluru

Coorg, the slow way

Misty coffee estates, a stream to splash in and a ridge-top view most visitors never see. Paced for your dog, with nothing longer than a few hours at a stretch.

Day 1

Into the coffee hills

Pickup from home, an easy drive with sniff breaks along the way, and a dog-friendly estate stay waiting at the end. Evening walk between the coffee rows.

Day 2

A stream of their own

A quiet stretch of water for paddlers and swimmers alike, then a long, lazy afternoon on the verandah.

Day 3

The view from the ridge

An early ride up to a ridge-top view over the hills, back before the heat. Old estate stories over dinner.

Day 4

Home, happily tired

A slow breakfast, one last walk and the drive back, dropped right at your door.

Where you'll stay

We pick the stay for your dates from estates we've visited ourselves. Every one has space for dogs to run and staff who are glad to see them.

forest

Coffee-estate homestay

A family-run planter's home with walking trails through the estate.

cottage

Riverside cottage

Private cottages near the water, for dogs who live to swim.

Good to know

  • The drive from Bengaluru takes about 5–6 hours with breaks.
  • Coorg is cool and misty from October to March, and very wet in the monsoon (June to September).
  • Your dog should be vaccinated and have a tick and flea treatment before travel. We'll send a short checklist when you book.
