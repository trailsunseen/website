Your privacy

Last updated: [DATE]. Written in plain words. If anything is unclear, ask us.

What we collect

The details you send us when you enquire or book: your name, phone number, email, trip preferences and information about your dog and family.

Why we use it

Only to plan and run your trip, to contact you about it, and, if you agree, to tell you about new trips.

Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to run your trip: the stay, the driver and, if needed, a vet. We never sell your details.

How long we keep it

For as long as we need it to run trips for you, or as the law requires. Ask us at any time to see or delete your details.
