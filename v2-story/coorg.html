Coorg, the slow way.

Misty coffee estates, a clear stream to swim in and a ridge-top sunrise most visitors never see. Three nights from Bengaluru, at your dog's pace.

Plan your trip

Four days, planned around your dog.

Nothing runs longer than a few hours at a stretch. Your dog's size, energy and feelings about water decide the walks, the swims and the pace.

  1. Day 1

    Into the coffee hills.

    We pick you up from home. An easy drive with sniff breaks along the way, then you arrive at an estate stay where dogs are genuinely welcome. In the evening, a walk between the coffee rows.

  2. Day 2

    A stream of their own.

    A quiet, clear stretch of water for paddlers and swimmers alike, checked by us first. Then a long, lazy afternoon on the verandah while everyone dries off.

  3. Day 3

    The view from the ridge.

    Up early for a drive to a ridge-top view over the hills, to watch the valley fill with light. Back before the day warms up.

  4. Day 4

    Home, happily tired.

    A slow breakfast, one last walk through the estate and the drive home. We drop you at your door, everyone happily tired.

If your dog is tired or unwell, the plan changes. No pushing on.

You pack the leash. We handle the rest.

  1. 1

    Your own car and driver.

    From your door and back again. Your dog rides with you, never in a crate or the boot.

  2. 2

    A stay we've walked through ourselves.

    We visit every stay to check the fences, the floors and how the staff are with dogs. Your dog sleeps in your room, with you.

  3. 3

    Every meal, sorted.

    Breakfast to dinner is planned for the whole trip. No hunting for a dog-friendly table.

  4. 4

    A vet on call, day and night.

    Someone who picks up at 2 a.m. If your dog needs to be seen, your driver takes you straight there.

Where you'll stay.

We choose the stay for your dates from places we've visited ourselves.

  • A coffee-estate homestay

    A family-run planter's home with walking trails through the estate and coffee straight from it.

  • A riverside cottage

    A private cottage close to the water, for dogs who live to swim.

On the road.

  • Regular stops for water and a stretch, away from highway traffic.
  • Your dog is never left alone in a parked car.
  • We only suggest a swim where we've seen the water and the banks.

Good to know.

The drive
About 5 to 6 hours each way from Bengaluru, with breaks.
When to go
Cool and misty from October to March. Very wet in the monsoon, June to September.
Your dog
Vaccinations up to date, and a tick and flea treatment before you travel.
The price
It depends on your dates, how many of you travel and the stay. You get a clear, all-in price before you pay anything. Children are very welcome.

Ready when your dog is.

Share your dates and we'll call you within a day with the plan and the price. Nothing to pay until you've seen both.
