Fun first. Never at their expense.

Plans that bend

Tired dog, rainy day, upset tummy? We change the plan. You never have to push on.

Before the trip

We ask about your dog before we plan anything: size, age, energy, how they travel, whether they swim, how they feel about strangers and other dogs. A nervous senior and a bouncy one-year-old need very different trips.

We'll send you a short checklist too: vaccinations up to date, a tick and flea treatment, their usual food, a favourite blanket.

On the road

Your dog rides with you, never in a crate or the boot. We stop regularly for water and a stretch, at places away from highway traffic, and we never leave a dog alone in a parked car.

At the stay

We only use stays we've visited ourselves. We look at the fences, the floors, the stairs, the water and, most of all, at how the people treat dogs.

If something goes wrong

We know the nearest vet at every stop and how to get there. If your dog is tired, unwell or just not enjoying something, we change the plan. You never have to push on.

Ready when your dog is.

Tell us about them and we'll shape a trip around them. A real person replies within a day.
